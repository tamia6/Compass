指南针、风水罗盘和水平仪的使用帮助。

切换模式与外观

点击右上角按钮，依次切换普通指南针、风水罗盘和水平仪。点击左上角切换浅色或深色；长按该按钮可选择跟随系统。应用默认跟随系统语言。

位置、街道或海拔没有显示

在 iPhone「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 定位服务」中找到本应用，允许使用期间访问位置，并按需打开「精确位置」。街道名称依赖网络及 Apple 地址解析服务；信号较弱时，请移至开阔处稍候。经纬度和海拔仅在普通模式显示，海拔精度取决于设备与定位条件。

指南针方向不准确

请远离磁性保护壳、磁铁、大型金属物和强电磁干扰。将手机平稳放置，按系统提示校准。应用在可用时显示真北方向，否则使用磁北方向。

水平仪如何使用

将手机平放在需要测量的表面。参照线变绿表示进入水平范围；黄色表示接近中心。进入水平范围时会提供触感反馈，实际效果受设备支持和系统设置影响。手机壳和摄像头凸起可能影响测量。

使用范围

本应用用于日常方向查看与辅助水平测量，不适用于安全关键导航或专业精密测量。风水罗盘展示传统方位标签。

ENGLISH

Support

Tap the top-right button to cycle through Compass, Feng Shui Compass and Level. Tap the top-left button to change appearance; long-press it to follow the system. App language follows your system preferences.

Location and direction

Enable location access while using the app in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services. Enable Precise Location if needed. Street names require a network connection and Apple’s reverse-geocoding service. Coordinates and elevation appear only in standard mode. Move away from magnets, magnetic cases and large metal objects if the heading seems incorrect. True north is used when available; otherwise the app uses magnetic north.

Level measurements

Place the phone flat on the surface. Green reference lines indicate the level range; yellow indicates near-center alignment. Haptic feedback depends on device support and system settings. Cases and camera protrusions may affect measurements. This app is not intended for safety-critical navigation or precision professional measurements. Feng Shui mode shows traditional directional labels.
