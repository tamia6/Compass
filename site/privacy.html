适用应用：指南针与水平仪（com.tamia.CompassReplica）

1. 我们如何处理数据

应用无需注册账号，不接入广告、行为分析或用户追踪 SDK。开发者不通过本应用将您的位置、方向或运动传感器读数上传至开发者服务器，也不建立位置历史记录。

2. 位置与街道名称

在您授权后，应用在使用期间通过 Apple 定位服务获取位置，用于显示经纬度、海拔、当前位置和真北方向。街道名称通过 Apple 的反向地理编码服务获取：系统可能向 Apple 发送坐标等请求数据以返回地址，该处理受 Apple 的隐私政策约束。此功能可能需要联网。我们不接收或保存这类服务请求。

3. 方向与运动传感器

应用使用设备方向及运动传感器数据，在设备上计算指南针朝向、倾角及水平状态，并触发触感反馈。传感器读数不由应用持久化保存；应用进入非活动状态时停止定位和运动更新。

4. 本地设置

显示模式和外观偏好保存在设备的系统偏好存储中，用于下次打开时恢复设置。应用不提供开发者账户同步。系统备份是否包含应用设置由您的 Apple 系统设置决定。

5. 您的选择

您可以在系统设置中撤回定位权限或关闭精确位置，相应的位置功能可能无法使用或精度降低。删除应用可移除其本地数据；已有系统备份需通过您的备份设置管理。

6. 技术支持与本网站

如果您主动通过邮件联系我们，我们会接收您提供的邮箱、消息和附件，仅用于处理反馈与相关沟通，并在不再需要时删除，法律要求保留的除外。请不要发送不必要的精确位置或其他敏感资料。

本网站由 Cloudflare Pages 托管。访问时，Cloudflare 可能为提供网络服务和安全防护处理 IP 地址、请求时间、浏览器信息等技术数据。网站不主动加入广告或访问分析脚本，也不设置用于追踪的 Cookie。Apple 和 Cloudflare 的独立处理分别适用其隐私政策：Apple、Cloudflare。

7. 政策更新与联系

功能或数据处理方式发生变化时，我们会更新此页面及生效日期。隐私问题请联系：tamia.here@gmail.com。

Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026. Developer: ming tan. Applies to Compass & Level (com.tamia.CompassReplica).

App data

No account is required. The app contains no advertising, behavioral analytics or tracking SDKs. The developer does not upload your location, heading or motion readings to a developer-operated server or maintain a location history.

Location and Apple services

With your permission, the app uses Apple location services while in use to display coordinates, elevation, location and true north. To obtain street names, Apple’s reverse-geocoding service may send coordinates and related request data to Apple. This processing is subject to Apple’s privacy policy and may require internet access. The developer does not receive or store those service requests.

Motion and preferences

Heading and motion data are processed on your device to calculate direction, tilt and level feedback. The app does not persist sensor readings and stops location and motion updates when inactive. Display-mode and appearance preferences are stored locally. There is no developer-provided account sync; system backups are controlled by your Apple settings.

Your controls

You can revoke location access or disable Precise Location in system settings; related features may stop working or become less precise. Deleting the app removes its local data. Manage existing backups separately in your backup settings.

Support and this website

If you email us, we receive the email address, message and attachments you choose to provide. We use them to handle your request and related correspondence and delete them when no longer needed, except where retention is legally required. Do not send unnecessary sensitive information.

This site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may process technical data such as IP addresses, request times and browser information to deliver and protect the site. We add no advertising or visitor analytics scripts and set no tracking cookies. Independent processing by Apple and Cloudflare is governed by their respective privacy policies linked above.

Updates and contact

We will update this page and its effective date when our features or data practices change. For privacy questions, contact tamia.here@gmail.com.
